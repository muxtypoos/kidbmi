# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (first release)
- Standalone paediatric calculator split out of Admix, with its own engine (`src/engine.js`) and UI.
- BMI, Peters lean body weight and extracellular fluid volume, RCPCH BMI centile band and chart (ages 2 to 20).
- Age is required. A result under 25% of total weight is shown in red and marked implausible.
- Only the Peters formula is offered for lean body weight (the adult formulas remain in the engine, unused by the screen).
- Added `selftest.csv` (generated by `scripts/selftest.js`): thousands of readable rows with an independent re-calculation, including growth-path, grid and extreme cases. `npm test` checks it.
- BMI now has its own large box showing the RCPCH band, the nearest centile line (with the BMI difference from it) and a ruler marking the BMI against the RCPCH centile lines. selftest.csv gained nearest-line columns with an independent check.
- Pink colour theme (header, buttons, selected states). Clinical band colours (green, amber, red) are unchanged.
- Underweight added. The centile data is now the exact RCPCH nine-line reference (0.4th to 99.6th), replacing the three digitised upper lines. Bands: very underweight (below 0.4th), underweight (below 2nd), within the usual range, overweight (91st), obese (98th), severely obese (99.6th). The nearest centile line is now chosen from all nine lines and the ruler shows them.
- Ruler: triangle marker, thinner bar, smaller tags on two alternating rows so they cannot overlap, and labels kept inside the picture. Smaller BMI text.
- Ruler: the nine centile notches are equally spaced (position between two notches is interpolated by BMI), with extra space at both ends. The Male button is blue when selected. "Peters formula" removed from the total body weight box. Stale comments and wording corrected.
