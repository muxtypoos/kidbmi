# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- **Morbidly obese** is now detected and shown: BMI at or above the RCPCH chart's +3.33 SD line (the chart prints "Morbidly obese" just above that line). The box shows the +3.33 SD value at the child's age. Severely obese remains 99.6th centile up to the +3.33 SD line. The +3.33 SD value is also in the centile list, the working, the copied summary and selftest.csv. It is not drawn on the ruler.
- The +3.33 SD line is not one of the nine published centile lines, so `scripts/make_centile_data.py` computes it from the RCPCH curves (Cole LMS form, fitted from the 0.4th, 50th and 99.6th lines; the nine lines refit to within 0.0005 BMI). It was checked by overlay on the printed RCPCH girls' and boys' charts, and selftest.csv re-derives it a second way (from the 2nd, 50th and 98th lines) and checks agreement within 0.01.

## 0.2.0 (review fixes)
- **Age** is now entered as years, months and days (months matter: about 1 in 10 test cases changed band within a year of age). Required.
- **Rounding:** BMI, centile values and differences round to the nearest 0.1 (before they rounded down, which could show a BMI equal to a line it was above). Lean weight and ages still round down. Total weight is shown as entered.
- Fixed "BMI is 0.0 above the 0.4th line". Fixed the light-mode header, which was always the dark maroon. "Show working" now prints weight and height unrounded so the sum can be checked by hand.
- Cautions: age over 20 (paediatric tools), under 1 year (Peters not verified in infants). A lean weight cut back to total weight now says "capped at total weight" in the box.
- A note near 4 years about the UK-WHO to UK90 reference change; a note that the ruler colours are for orientation; "(off chart)" on the chart marker when BMI is outside 8 to 50.
- Tap the RCPCH chart to enlarge it. Warnings are no longer re-announced to screen readers on every keystroke. Android: the working panels opened for printing close again when the app returns.
- Removed the four adult lean body weight formulas and other unused code from the engine (Peters is the only formula). Engine, tests, self-test and docs updated.
- New screen tests (`tests/ui.js`); the Admix comparison now announces loudly when it is skipped. README states the licence questions that are still open.

## 0.1.0 (first release)
- Standalone paediatric calculator split out of Admix, with its own engine (`src/engine.js`) and UI.
- BMI, Peters lean body weight and extracellular fluid volume, RCPCH BMI centile band and chart (ages 2 to 20).
- Age is required. A result under 25% of total weight is shown in red and marked implausible.
- Only the Peters formula is offered for lean body weight (the adult formulas remain in the engine, unused by the screen).
- Added `selftest.csv` (generated by `scripts/selftest.js`): thousands of readable rows with an independent re-calculation, including growth-path, grid and extreme cases. `npm test` checks it.
- BMI now has its own large box showing the RCPCH band, the nearest centile line (with the BMI difference from it) and a ruler marking the BMI against the RCPCH centile lines. selftest.csv gained nearest-line columns with an independent check.
- Pink colour theme (header, buttons, selected states). Clinical band colours (green, amber, red) are unchanged.
- Underweight added. The centile data is now the exact RCPCH nine-line reference (0.4th to 99.6th), replacing the three digitised upper lines. Bands: very underweight (below 0.4th), underweight (below 2nd), within the usual range, overweight (91st), obese (98th), severely obese (99.6th). The nearest centile line is now chosen from all nine lines and the ruler shows them.
- Ruler: triangle marker, thinner bar, smaller tags on two alternating rows so they cannot overlap, and labels kept inside the picture. Smaller BMI text.
- Ruler: the nine centile notches are equally spaced (position between two notches is interpolated by BMI), with extra space at both ends. The Male button is blue when selected. "Peters formula" removed from the total body weight box. Stale comments and wording corrected.
- Print button (header and bottom): compacts the results, ruler, RCPCH chart and working onto one A4 page, with a patient line and date. In the Android app it calls the Capacitor PrintPlugin, as Admix does. Removed the sentence saying lean weight can differ by several kilograms.
- Ruler: three low-end segments to mirror the three high-end ones (below 0.4th, 0.4th to 2nd, 2nd to 9th). The 2nd to 9th segment is a light blue marker only: the band text still says within the usual range from the 2nd centile.
