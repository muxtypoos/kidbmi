About and disclaimer

KidBMI calculates body mass index, estimates lean body weight with the Peters formula (derived in children), and places children aged 2 to 20 years on the RCPCH BMI centile chart. It is decision support only. It does not replace clinical judgement or local policy, and the formulas are estimates that can differ by several kilograms. Check any value that will be used for dosing against your own guideline.

Safeguards: if the formula gives a lean weight under 25% of total weight (or no valid number), it is marked implausible (shown in red) and should not be relied on. Lean weight is never shown above total weight or below zero.
